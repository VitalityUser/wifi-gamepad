# 🍎 ArachnidPad Companion Server for macOS (MacBook / iMac / Mac mini)

Use your Android phone as a high-performance, ultra-low latency Wi-Fi game controller and trackpad on macOS.

---

## ⚡ Quick Start on Mac

### 1. Download & Launch
1. Double-click `start_server.command` (or run `python3 server_mac.py` in Terminal).
2. The ArachnidPad server dashboard will open and display your Mac's Wi-Fi IP address.

### 2. Connect Your Phone
1. Connect your Android phone and Mac to the same Wi-Fi network.
2. Launch the **ArachnidPad** app on your phone.
3. The app will auto-discover your Mac and connect in 1 tap!

---

## 🔒 macOS Accessibility Permission (Required for Game Inputs)
macOS requires Accessibility permissions for apps to simulate keyboard and mouse events:
1. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility**.
2. Enable the toggle next to **Terminal** (or your Python runtime).
